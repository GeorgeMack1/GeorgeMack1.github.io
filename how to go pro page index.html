How to Go Pro

  1. Master Core Probabilities: Memorize basic odds and track your hands to build speed and accuracy.
  2. Study Behavioral Biases: Learn pattern recognition, such as how players react after a win or a loss.
  3. Enter Competitions: Join local or regional tournaments to get comfortable playing under pressure.
  4. Build Unpredictable Patterns: Train yourself to mix up your throws so opponents cannot read your routine.
  5. Analyze Match Film: Watch competitive games to study high-level tactics and physical tells.
