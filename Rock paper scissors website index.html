How to win in Rock, Paper, Scissors

Rock, Paper, Scissors is a simple game where players chose between either rock, paper or scissors in attempt to win.

Top tips on Starting a Mactch:
  • Know your opponent, satistics show that males start with rock 40% of the time.
  • Less expirenced players will also start with rock more often. Experienced players know that rock is commonly used, so scissors is a good first throw.
  • If you tell your opponent your move before hand you can use double reverse pyscology, by doing the move you announced.
Things to Keep in Mind during a Match
  • The win-stay, lose-shift strategy is where if you win you should assume that your opponent will switch. If you lose then you should switch to the move that was not played in the last round.
  • Throwing the same move three times in a row is considered predictible and that is why most don't play this or defend against it.
